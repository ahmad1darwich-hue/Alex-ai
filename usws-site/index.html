Why a specialist

Northern Beaches & North Shore gardens aren't like the rest of Sydney

Coastal blocks, sandstone and leafy established gardens need building methods that last. It's most of what we do — so we build for it from the start.

🌊

Coastal conditions

Salt spray, sandy soils and exposed aspects eat cheap fixings and the wrong plants. We use marine-grade hardware, salt-tolerant planting and materials chosen to handle the beaches — from Manly to Palm Beach.

📐

Slopes, sandstone & drainage

So many blocks here sit on slopes and sandstone. We engineer retaining and terracing properly, and get the drainage and stormwater right behind the wall — not just in front of it — so it holds for the long run.

🌳

Established North Shore gardens

Leafy Lower and Upper North Shore blocks come with mature trees, heritage feel and tight access. We work cleanly around what's there, protect the property, and finish to suit the street.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes — every quote starts with a free, no-obligation on-site visit so we can measure up and give you a clear, fixed price in writing. No pressure, no surprises later.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold NSW contractor licence 293802C, are fully insured for residential and commercial work, and build to Sydney and Australian building and safety standards. ABN 20 386 532 878.

Do you really specialise in the Northern Beaches and North Shore?

It's most of what we do. We build for coastal conditions — salt-tolerant planting, marine-grade fixings, sandstone and sloping-block retaining with proper drainage — and we know the access and street character of the Lower and Upper North Shore.

Do you handle retaining walls and drainage on sloping blocks?

Yes — it's one of our core jobs here. We build sandstone, besser and timber retaining and terracing, and get the drainage and stormwater done properly behind the wall so it lasts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope — a lawn or small paving job can be a day or two, while a full landscape build may run a week or more. Your written quote includes a clear timeline before we start.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes — we work with homeowners and businesses, from a single garden to larger commercial landscaping, paving and concreting projects.

How do you quote — is the price fixed?

After the free site visit we send one clear fixed price for the whole job. If you later change the scope, we'll quote that change up front too — you'll always know the price before work happens.

How soon will you get back to me?

Call any time on 0434 449 997 or 0414 922 246. Quote requests and emails are answered within one business day — often the same day.
