Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes — every quote starts with a free, no-obligation site visit so we can measure up and give you a clear, fixed price.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. NSW contractor licence 293802C, fully insured, and we work to Sydney and Australian building and safety standards. ABN 20 386 532 878.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope — a small job can take a day, while a full landscape build may take a week or more. Your quote includes a clear timeline.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes — we work with homeowners and businesses, from single gardens to larger commercial landscaping and concreting projects.

How soon will you get back to me?

Call us any time on 0434 449 997 or 0414 922 246. For emails and quote requests, we reply within one business day — often the same day.
