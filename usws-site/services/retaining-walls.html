Good to know

Retaining Walls — common questions

Do I need council approval for a retaining wall?

It depends on height and location. Lower walls often don't, taller ones usually do and may need engineering. We'll tell you where your wall sits and handle approvals and engineering where they're needed.

Sandstone, besser or timber — which is best?

It comes down to look, height and budget. Sandstone suits heritage and coastal blocks, besser is strong and cost-effective (and can be rendered), timber is quick and natural. We'll recommend the right fit on the site visit.

Why do retaining walls fail?

Almost always water pressure from poor drainage behind the wall. We build the drainage properly behind every wall — that's the part cheaper jobs skip.
