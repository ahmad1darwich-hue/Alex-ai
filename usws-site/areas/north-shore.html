Landscaping built for North Shore homes

North Shore gardens are often established, leafy and set on sloping, sandstone ground — and the homes deserve work that matches. We build sandstone and block retaining walls, terraces and steps that tame a level change; travertine, sandstone and large-format paving for courtyards and entertaining areas; hardwood decks and pergolas; and new driveways in decorative or exposed concrete.

One licensed team handles the whole project across the North Shore: design, excavation, drainage, retaining, concreting, paving, decking, turf and planting — including shade-tolerant garden beds under established trees. Free on-site visit, fixed written quote, built to Australian standards.

  • Sandstone and block retaining walls, terracing, steps and drainage on sloping blocks
  • Courtyards and entertaining areas in travertine, sandstone and large-format paving
  • Hardwood and merbau decks, pergolas and pool surrounds
  • Driveways, paths and slabs in decorative, exposed or stencil concrete
  • Turf, shade-tolerant planting and feature gardens for established leafy streets
  • Homes in Mosman, Cremorne, Neutral Bay, North Sydney, Lane Cove, Chatswood, Willoughby, Roseville, Lindfield, Killara, Gordon, St Ives and Wahroonga
